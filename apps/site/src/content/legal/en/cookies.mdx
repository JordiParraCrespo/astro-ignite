# Cookie Policy

This page describes the storage that [YOUR COMPANY NAME] uses on this site and how you can control it.

## What we use

This site uses **`localStorage`** for two purposes:

| Key              | Purpose                                                          | Lifetime                             |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `cookie-consent` | Remembers whether you accepted or declined non-essential storage | Until you clear your browser storage |
| `theme`          | Remembers your light/dark/system theme preference                | Until you clear your browser storage |

Neither of these are cookies in the strict HTTP sense — they live only in your browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

## Optional analytics

When you accept on the cookie banner, we load [YOUR ANALYTICS PROVIDER]. By default this is **Plausible Analytics**, which:

- Does not set cookies.
- Does not collect personal data.
- Records aggregate page-view events.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing this site's `localStorage` for `cookie-consent`. The banner will reappear on your next visit.
